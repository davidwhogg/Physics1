\begin{problem} (From Problem Set 8)

and $X, \phi$ that make them identical?

\begin{problem} (From the Recitation on spacetime diagrams)
Draw a spacetime diagram for your own rest-frame.  On the spacetime
diagram, show your own world-line.
Imagine there is a galaxy flying away from you with a velocity $v =
0.5\,c$. When the galaxy is moving away, it sends back to you a light
signal every $c\,T'=1\,\m$ (as recorded in the galaxy's rest frame).
Draw the world-line of this galaxy on your spacetime diagram and mark
the events corresponding to the departures of three of these signals from the
galaxy, and the events corresponding to the arrivals of those three signals at
you, and the world lines of the signals. Draw at least three such signals.
\end{problem}
